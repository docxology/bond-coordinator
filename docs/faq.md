# FAQ — bond-coordinator

## What is this package?

`bond-coordinator` is the **registry and situation room** for the BOND suite
(Layer 2).  It records, audits, and reports on the 27 film packages; it
implements no film algorithms.

## Why is there no `experiment:` block in manuscript/config.yaml?

The coordinator has no analysis outputs to gate on.  `generate_variables(...,
require_outputs=...)` accepts the flag for pipeline parity but never gates on
generated files — a deliberate divergence from the exemplar's strict
analysis-to-manuscript contract (documented in
`docs/architecture.md` and `manuscript/05_experimental_setup.md`).

## Why are all packages `scaffolded` with `coverage=None`?

Because that is the honest, measured state: the fleet is at phase-0 scaffold.
Status/coverage are only updated from real gate output, never hand-asserted
(see `registry.py` docstring).

## Where do the mission codenames come from?

Assigned by mission control from each film's canonical plot element (e.g.
Goldfinger → `GRAND SLAM`).  They are provisional until a film package declares
its own codename via `bond-api` at merge time.

## When will `bond-api` / `bond-utilities` be used?

When Layer 0–1 are built.  Until then `src/bond_coordinator/types.py` is a
pure-core mirror of the protocol shapes; the swap is a single import (see
`TODO.md`).  `scripts/` already probes for `bond_api` and reports
`dependency_ready`.

## Why is `output/` empty / not in git?

`output/` is gitignored and disposable.  Everything under it is regenerated by
`scripts/` and `z_generate_manuscript_variables.py` — never hand-edit it.

## Are there figures?

No.  The coordinator has no plotting; the manuscript defines no figures and no
equations (see `manuscript/SYNTAX.md`).
